Our Love Story

I met Akira in February 2013 while I was a working student at Jollibee Trece during my college years. At first, I wasn't interested in him, and he didn't show any obvious interest in me either. He was one of the Counter Trainers, and to me, everything was just normal. 😅


Then, our managers started teasing us, saying that Akira had a crush on me. Because of that, they often paired us together to achieve our sales targets. Looking back, we made a great team. We were competitive, worked well together, and consistently hit our goals. As a reward, we would receive a TLC Burger Meal almost every day. Whenever we finished our shifts together, Akira would always give me the vegetables from his burger because he didn't like them. It was a small gesture, but our story hadn't really begun yet. ☺️


Since we lived along the same route, we often travelled home together. Whenever we worked the closing shift, we usually missed the last jeepney, so we ended up walking home instead. Those walks allowed me to see the kind of person Akira truly was. Even though he liked me, he never took advantage of the situation. He was always respectful, thoughtful, and a true gentleman.
